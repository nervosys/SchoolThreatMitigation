# Version 17.0 · Threat-to-Countermeasure Engineering

Release date: 26 October 2026. Single-version ZIP, replacing Version 16.0 Revision D as current. Does not include earlier versions, historical video, stale QA, or unrendered TTS labeled as finished.

- Integrated new Section 20 into revised Field Guide.
- Added 18 public threat scenario cards and 15 control requirements with owners, non-tactical verification and hard-stop gates.
- Added a five-gate independent qualification framework, concise executive decision brief, 90-day pilot plan, G0 constitutional charter and adaptive technology report.
- Rebuilt an editable Version 17.0 slide deck with scenario tracing and control validation.
- Preserved general prevention, AI, cyber, rights, canine, medical and early-response discussions while clearly distinguishing demonstrated from speculative applications.

**This release has not been operationally field-tested.**

## Engine 0.4.0 RC10: numeric MCP schema conformance (2026-10-31)

Final RC10 qualifications have separate source/executable-bound companion records; only passing records establish execution. No independent review, source approval, legal certification or deployment authorization is granted.
