# Public information handling

Only general scenarios, controls and de-identified sample artifacts are in this repository. Do not add specific campus maps, door plans, camera locations, school network addresses, credentials, students' records, private reporting identities, detailed armed-response tactics, exploitable timing vulnerabilities or adversarial bypass instructions.

A separately controlled site-specific supplement should be subject to public-records and disclosure counsel, with minimum necessary access, a documented retention schedule and response to legal process. No voluntary family firearm-owner registry.
