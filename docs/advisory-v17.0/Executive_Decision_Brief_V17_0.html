Board decision brief · Version 17.0

School Safety: Specific Threats, Measurable Countermeasures

Defensive readiness without compromising constitutional rights or public trust

Approve a scenario-based qualification program, not a technology shopping list. Assess credible threats to students, protect lawful ownership, and fund only controls that demonstrate a protective purpose and do not create unacceptable new hazards.

Five immediate decisions

  1. Require a traceable threat register. Give every concern an observable condition, independent corroboration, response authority, outcome metric and sunset criterion.
  2. Support voluntary secure storage. Offer families storage education and equipment referrals without collecting firearm-owner identities.
  3. Verify initial-minutes protective capability. Independently exercise alerts, classroom egress, accessible communications, bleeding control, and dispatch coordination.
  4. Separate established controls from exploratory technology. Permit small offline pilots of sensors, canines and AI, but do not equate promising capabilities with verified reductions in shooting casualties.
  5. Make G0 governance non-waivable. Set clear legal authority, restrictions on sharing student records, independent complaint review, and penalties for retaliatory or selective use.

Deployment rule: Zero tolerance for illegal authority, blocked egress, unreviewed force decisions or disclosure of student records outside lawful purpose. Automated publication QA does not equal live-site qualification.
