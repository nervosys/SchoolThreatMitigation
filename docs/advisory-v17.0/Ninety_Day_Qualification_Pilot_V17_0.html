90-Day Threat-to-Control Qualification Pilot

Designed for evidence generation, not automatic operational certification

Day 0 — Establish decision rights

Board sponsor designates incident commander, threat assessment chair, legal/privacy authority, fire/building AHJ liaison, disability-access coordinator, EMS liaison and independent evaluator. All participants sign a minimum-necessary information protocol. Secure records remain separate from voluntary safe-storage participation.

Ownership and safety review

Use one accountable owner per control. Required co-approvers for high-consequence changes are the district incident command, qualified fire/AHJ reviewer where applicable, accessibility lead, privacy counsel, independent evaluator and, for police operations, the authorized law-enforcement agency. Community participation and an independent complaint channel are mandatory for legitimacy.

Qualification gates

  1. Necessity: why this threat is materially relevant and baseline safeguards insufficient.
  2. Safety: no egress trapping, unacceptable false alarms, added injury, or unmeasured cascading behavior.
  3. Rights: documented legal basis, no ownership registry, no ideological targeting or coercion, records purpose restrictions.
  4. Cyber: verified vendor update chain, segmentation and safe manual recovery.
  5. Independent evidence: test logs reproducible and decision signed by parties not financially dependent on selected vendor.
Mandatory STOP: unqualified armed activity, a proposed automatic use-of-force feature, unapproved student surveillance, an egress-related critical defect, unlawful sharing of education records, or any request to publish exploitable site details.

Metrics dashboard

Report validated risk-reduction proxies: opt-in uptake and comprehension; false-report escalation and correction; alert reach and latency distributions; successful accessible evacuation/egress checks; kit and skills readiness; cyber restoration outcomes; completed case reviews; community complaints and their resolution. Do not infer casualty reduction from pilot proxies alone.

Deliverable at day 90

An independent acceptance memo records for each scenario: approve low-risk measure / fund corrective action / limit to offline pilot / suspend or reject. Maintain a future review cadence driven by emerging threat evidence and control failures, not a calendar purchase quota.
