Washington implementation context

RCW 28A.320.124 requires school-security policies covering limits on SRO school discipline and family complaint processes. RCW 28A.320.1242 provides for annual review and adoption of agreements with law enforcement or security providers, involving students, parents and community members. RCW 9.41.360 creates liability in specified unsafe-storage situations but expressly does not mandate how or where every firearm must be stored. These statements are summaries, not legal advice; have Washington counsel verify current law for each deployment.

FERPA data firewall

Department of Education guidance permits disclosure to external threat-team members as school officials under particular conditions, but use is purpose-limited and redisclosure restricted. A police officer’s team participation is not a general gateway into ordinary police databases. Document lawful exceptions separately and log all disclosures.

Complaint and remedy protocol

  1. Accept school, independent and external complaints, with accessible and confidential options.
  2. Record allegations without presuming guilt; preserve evidence and exculpatory material.
  3. Investigate through an independent reviewer when police or district leaders are implicated.
  4. Notify families of determinations and available review or correction rights as applicable.
  5. Publish anonymized complaint statistics, reasons for policy changes and suspension decisions.
Oversight stop rules: retaliatory referral, selection based on protected speech or identity, unlawful searches or record transfers, force beyond authorized roles, or AI-enabled automatic adverse action.
